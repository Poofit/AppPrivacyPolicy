Magicday privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Magicday (“the app”) is an itinerary planner for Orlando theme-park trips. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in the app (trips, days, plans, notes, cover photos and settings) is stored only on your device. We do not run a server, there are no accounts, and we do not receive, collect or have access to your trips.

If you export a backup or share an itinerary image, the file goes where you choose to send it, using Android’s share sheet. We do not see it.

If you choose a cover photo, the app keeps a private copy in its own storage. The copy is deleted with the trip or when you uninstall the app.

Ads

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google may collect and use your device’s advertising ID, IP address, and information about how you interact with ads. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice later in Settings › Ad privacy choices.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads). Google’s own policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Buying Premium removes ads.

Purchases

Premium is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google Play tells the app whether your account owns Premium so that it can unlock the features.

Children

Magicday is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your choices

  • Delete your data: uninstall the app, or delete trips inside it.
  • Ads: change your consent in the app’s Settings, or opt out of personalisation in Android settings.

Changes
